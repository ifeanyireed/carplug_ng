01 · Foundation

Vision & core philosophy

Most car sites in Nigeria sell exposure — a photo, a price, a phone number, and a prayer. Verza doesn't sell listings. It sells certainty: independently verified condition, fair-price scoring, and a controlled connection to the seller, so nobody drives across Lagos only to discover the car was never what the photos promised.

Not this

Photo-and-phone-number listing board

Jiji, Facebook Marketplace, and WhatsApp groups — anyone can post anything, buyers verify nothing until they've already made the trip, and trust is entirely improvised.

This

Discovery, verification & connection engine

Every serious vehicle carries a Trust Tier, a technician-filed Vehicle Health Report, and a Price Score before a buyer ever calls the seller — the platform controls the lead, not just the listing.

"How do we let a buyer know exactly what they're getting before they've driven anywhere to see it?" — the single question every screen in Verza must answer.

Verification

Independent technicians, not the seller, decide what condition a car is actually in.

Transparency

Documentation status and price fairness shown plainly, before contact happens.

Control

The platform routes every lead — sellers and technicians never bypass it for free.

Confidence

The feeling that makes someone buy a car they've never personally driven off a lot.

Foundation

Target audience

Verza is built for the Nigerian buyer spending ₦3m–₦50m on a used car who has been burned before — not the casual browser scrolling for entertainment.

Buyers

  • → Age 28–55, first car or trading up, cash or bank-financed
  • → Based in Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, and other urban centers
  • → Has visited multiple car lots and been misled at least once
  • → Wants a mechanic's honest opinion but doesn't have one they trust

Sellers & dealers

  • → Independent car lots and multi-brand dealers across major cities
  • → Private owners selling one vehicle, often for the first time
  • → Want serious buyer leads, not tire-kickers and time-wasters
  • → Willing to pay for verified exposure once trust is proven
What the product must make buyers feel
"I know what's wrong with it.""This price is fair." "Nobody's hiding anything.""I didn't waste my Saturday." "I can trust this seller.""I'm in control of who contacts me."
02 · People

Roles & responsibilities

Four roles run Verza. Sellers cover both private owners and dealers — their dashboards diverge once a shop is involved.

Buyer

The searcher

Discovers, filters, compares, saves, requests inspections, contacts sellers, and tracks price drops on saved cars.

Seller / Dealer

Private owner or shop

Lists vehicles, uploads documents and media, manages leads and messages, and runs a shop storefront if a dealer.

Technician

The verifier

Accepts inspection requests, runs the structured checklist, files the Vehicle Health Report, and builds a rating.

Admin

Platform control

Verifies sellers and technicians, moderates listings, oversees lead routing, and manages payments and disputes.

Product

Core modules

The systems that actually deliver the "trust layer" promise — expanded from concept to feature list.

Trust Tier & verification engine

Foundation

Every listing climbs a five-level ladder rather than being automatically "verified" on day one — this is what makes the badge mean something.

  • Seller Listed
  • Documents Uploaded
  • Platform Verified
  • Technician Inspected
  • Premium Verified
  • Tier shown on every card and detail page
  • Filter search by minimum tier
  • Re-verification triggers on price or ownership change

Inspection flow & technician routing

Differentiator

The strongest wedge against classifieds sites — buyers get an independent technician's word, not the seller's.

  • Tiered inspections — Standard, Premium, Comprehensive, priced separately
  • Routing engine — matches by location, availability, rating, experience, and current load
  • Preferred / Premium / Platform Certified technician hierarchy
  • Structured checklist — engine, transmission, exterior, interior, suspension, documentation
  • Vehicle Health Report — scored, photo/video/audio backed, shareable

Price intelligence

AI layer

Every vehicle carries a Price Score against a live market range for its make, model, year, and condition — fair, above market, or a deal.

  • Market range computed from comparable active and sold listings
  • Plain-language read: "Suitable for purchase, subject to negotiation on identified defects"
  • Feeds Compare and the AI concierge match score

Geo-privacy

Public vs. exact location

Public area shown for discovery ("Lekki Phase 1"); exact address revealed only once a viewing or inspection is booked and the seller accepts.

Masked seller contact

Lead control

Phone numbers appear partially masked; Contact Seller opens an in-platform conversation the platform can track and monetize.

Dealer shop system

UGC growth engine

Every dealer gets a storefront with logo, hours, rating, and inventory — the marketplace grows because dealers manage their own shops.

Basic ShopPro ShopPremium Shop Inventory filtersShop analytics

Lead routing engine

Buyer requests are matched to sellers and technicians by location, availability, rating, and performance — free and paid lead tiers give the platform its core revenue lever.

Concierge & sell/trade-in

"Find It For Me" sources vehicles the buyer can't find on-platform; "Sell My Car" gives owners a market estimate and routes offers from dealers.

Engineering

AI layer

AI exists to translate mechanical and market data into a plain-language verdict a non-mechanic can act on. Every AI feature routes through a shared orchestrator, not disconnected integrations.

AI jobTriggerInputOutput
Price scoringListing published or price changedMake, model, year, mileage, condition, comparable listingsMarket range and fair/above/below rating
Vehicle analysisInspection report filedStructured checklist + technician notesPlain-language summary and estimated repair cost range
Concierge matchingBuyer submits "Find It For Me"Budget, purpose, location, preferencesRanked matches from live listings and dealer inventory
Technician routingInspection requestedLocation, availability, rating, current loadRanked technician recommendations
Document assistDocument uploadedCustoms / registration / particulars scanOCR extraction and completeness flag for admin review

All jobs run asynchronously so a slow generation never blocks a buyer's or technician's UI — they get a notification when output is ready.

Business

Go-to-market strategy

Lagos-first, technician-network-led, and anchored on a wedge most listing sites don't have: independently verifiable trust.

Channel 01

Technician network recruitment

Recruit and certify independent auto technicians and workshops first — inspection supply is the credibility engine everything else depends on.

Channel 02

Dealer lot onboarding

Onboard established Lagos car lots with free Basic Shops for the first cohort, converting to paid once leads prove out.

Channel 03

Trust-content marketing

Publish real inspection teardown videos and "we caught this before you bought it" stories — the report itself is the marketing asset.

Channel 04

Referral & buyer incentives

Discounted first inspection for buyers referred by a friend — word of mouth around a good/bad purchase story travels fast.

Channel 05

SME & agency network

Introduce Verza to Reed Breed Technologies' existing Nigerian SME client base as a first source of dealer partners.

Channel 06

Fleet & corporate partnerships

Sell verified sourcing and inspection bundles to companies replacing or expanding vehicle fleets.

Risks & mitigation

The honest list — including the ways trust itself can be gamed.

RiskSeverityMitigation
Technician collusion or falsified inspection reportsHighRandom audit re-inspections, photo/video evidence requirements, technician rating decay on disputed reports
Buyers and sellers taking the deal off-platform to avoid feesHighMasked contact until a paid inspection or lead is triggered, value-add (trust, escrow-style fee holding) strong enough to justify staying on-platform
Chicken-and-egg — not enough verified inventory to attract buyers, or vice versaHighManually seed one city with hand-vetted dealer inventory before opening self-serve listing
Seller document fraud (forged customs or registration papers)MediumAI document assist flags inconsistencies, human admin review before "Platform Verified" status
Inconsistent inspection quality across techniciansMediumStandardized checklist, mandatory certification, buyer feedback rolled into technician rating
Low dealer compliance with document upload requirementsMediumTier gating — undocumented listings rank lower and can't reach Premium Verified
Data protection compliance (NDPR) for location and identity dataMediumNDPR-aligned data handling review, exact-location masking by default, clear consent flows
Rising infra cost as photo/video inspection media growsLowCDN + tiered object storage, adaptive media compression
